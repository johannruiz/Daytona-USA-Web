On phone: drag the left pad to steer; hold GAS or BRAKE with your right thumb. Tap + / − to shift and VIEW to change camera. COIN adds credit; START begins a race. GAS also confirms selections. ☰ pauses; ♫ toggles sound; ⋯ reveals F2 and arcade menu controls. Landscape works best.

F2Opens the original arcade settings. F / S moves the cursor, D / S changes a value, and Enter selects. Choose EXIT to return. F3 is the service button.
